## Purpose

The site for Weft 2027, a two-day gathering of weavers, dyers and block printers
at The Calico Shed, a converted 1931 weaving shed in Ahmedabad, on 20 and 21
February 2027. It is run by a trust of the craftspeople who teach at it.

The site has one job: turn someone who has heard about Weft into someone holding
a pass, and make two days in Ahmedabad feel worth a train fare. The programme,
the venue and the contact page exist to remove a reason not to come.

## Users

Textile designers, studio owners, fashion and design students, and people who
collect handloom. Mostly 22 to 45, across India's larger cities. They read on
phones, often late in the evening, deciding whether ₹6,000 and a weekend are
worth it. A second audience is craftspeople applying to teach next year.

They have seen a hundred craft-fair sites with a terracotta palette and a stock
photo of hands on a pot. They want the dates, the price, what they will actually
get to do, and a working way to pay, inside one scroll.

## Brand

Two natural dyes carry the identity. Indigo is the ground: the gathering's
biggest floor is an indigo vat, and the drenched bands read like cloth come out of
it. Turmeric is the one action color, the yellow of a warp thread on a loom. The
signature is a woven selvedge where one band meets the next.

The type is Indian: Rozha One from Indian Type Foundry, based in Ahmedabad, for
display, and Anek Latin from EK Type, Mumbai, for everything else.

## Tone

Plain and particular. Say what happens on each floor, who teaches, what lunch is.
Numbers and names over adjectives. No "celebrating heritage", no "artisanal
journey", no exclamation marks. Warm the way a craftsperson is warm when they
explain their work: exact, and a little proud of it.

## Anti-references

- The craft-fair reflex: terracotta and cream, a hand-drawn leaf, a script font.
- Editorial-magazine restraint: italic serif headlines, mono labels, no photographs.
- Heritage-tourism sites: sepia filters, "timeless tradition", a map pin icon.
- Any hero built from a big number and a gradient.

## Strategic principles

1. **Show the work.** Every section carries a real photograph of the craft it
   describes. A colored block where a photograph belongs is a bug.
2. **The price is never more than one scroll away.** Every page links to the
   passes, and the passes page states today's price, not a range.
3. **One bold move.** The indigo drench and the selvedge are the identity.
   Everything around them stays quiet.
4. **Nothing invented.** A fact the client has not confirmed is marked as to be
   announced, never filled in.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every text and background pair in `tokens.css` is checked at 4.5:1
or better. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and every reveal renders its
content with JavaScript off.
